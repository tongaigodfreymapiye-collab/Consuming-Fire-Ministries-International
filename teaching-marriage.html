What Is Marriage?

By Apostle T. G. Mapiye

The Bible in the book of Genesis tells us that for marriage to take place a man and a woman must leave their families of origin in order to stick to each other like glue saying, “Therefore a man shall leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and they shall become one flesh.” (Gen 2:24). Jesus when asked about divorce said, “Have you not read that he who created them from the beginning made them male and female, and said, ‘Therefore a man shall leave his father and his mother and hold fast to his wife, and the two shall become one flesh’? So they are no longer two but one flesh. What therefore God has joined together, let not man separate.” (Mat 19:4-6)

Marriage is the coming together of a male and a female to form a family. Marriage is spiritually binding before God and involves God in the joining of a man and a woman into an inseparable bond. We do not advise young men and women to have close relationships with the opposite sex, which are not intended for marriage, as these boyfriends and girlfriends commonly involve themselves in immoral vices such as fornication. Hence, we caution that such relationships should be entered into solely for the purpose of marriage, and that the period for developing the relationship before the marriage (traditionally referred to as courtship) should not be too long.

Reasons for marriage

Companionship (Genesis 2:18-25)

God made the woman to be with the man as a companion.

Abstaining from fornication (1 Corinthians 7:2)

Marriage is the only legitimate avenue provided by God for a child of God to satisfy their sexual desires without committing sin. The Bible says in 1 Corinthians 7:9 “…it is better to marry than to burn with passion.” Passion is neither a demon nor a sin. As a youth grows up, there comes a time where passion is aroused and they desire to express these strong feelings with the opposite sex. The Bible warns, “Flee from sexual immorality. Every other sin a person commits is outside the body, but the sexually immoral person sins against his own body.” (1 Corinthians 6:18).

The plan of God for the human body and God’s design for the body and for sexual purity is clearly outlined in the Bible. The body was never meant for casual sex, sexual immorality or to be infected with sexually transmitted diseases, unwanted pregnancies and immorality. Every other sin a person commits is outside the body, but the sexually immoral person sins against his own body. When an individual Christian commits sexual immorality, it disgraces the entire body of Christ. This is why Christian single people must remain sexually pure and avoid falling into sexual immorality.

To become one flesh (Genesis 2:23-24)

In marriage, God has called two to become one, to be united in attitude, mind, vision, and direction.

Wrong reasons to get married

Trial and error

The moment you enter marriage with the attitude ‘if it does not work, I will get a divorce’, be sure that your reason to get married is wrong.

Sometimes it begins with getting into a wrong relationship more than just marrying for the wrong reasons. There are some red flags that an unmarried person should consider.

One party is born again (Christian) and the other party is not born again (Unbeliever)

“Stop forming inappropriate relationships with unbelievers. Can right and wrong be partners? Can light have anything in common with darkness? Can Christ agree with the devil? Can a believer share life with an unbeliever? Can God's temple contain false gods? Clearly, we are the temple of the living God. As God said, ‘I will live and walk among them. I will be their God, and they will be my people.’ The Lord says, ‘Get away from unbelievers. Separate yourselves from them. Have nothing to do with anything unclean. Then I will welcome you.’ The Lord Almighty says, ‘I will be your Father, and you will be my sons and daughters.’” (2 Cor 6:14-18)

If you are born again, you are not supposed to get into a relationship with a person who is not born again. God said, “Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers.” This is very important for Christian youths never to enter into a marriage relationship with an unbeliever. This is particularly important for ladies as in a marriage context you won’t always agree with every decision the man makes. You do not put yourself under the authority of someone who is not submitted to his Church, because if problems arise, you would have no one to escalate it to.

One party is too busy to be involved in Church

“One who is faithful in a very little is also faithful in much, and one who is dishonest in a very little is also dishonest in much.” (Luke 16:10)

Why would you as a youth want to marry someone who has no time for God and the Church? If someone is not fulfilling their call in God, why does he/she want to marry since marriage is to enable you serve God better? If a person’s commitment to Church cannot be ascertained, then it is better to wait until suitable adjustments are made. Why would a youth be so desperate to marry someone who is not concerned about the Church or who has not prioritized the Church?

One is already married to someone but is willing to divorce their spouse to accommodate you

You cannot break another home to establish your own. “…All who use a sword will be killed by a sword.” (Mat 26:52). Home breakers will also have their own homes broken by others. You cannot build a marriage by cheating first and then stealing the spouse of another. Falling in love with a married person is always sinful and wrong.

Someone who encourages you to lie to your leaders

It is very dangerous to think that marrying someone who encourages you to tell lies to your pastor and Church leaders will never lie to you or will be faithful to you. That person is a moving ‘time bomb’. If the person cajoles you to believe that their love for you supersedes the authority of the Church that kind of love is manipulative.

Someone who has no plan for survival

The Bible says, ‘if anyone will not work, neither let him eat’ (2 Thessalonians 3:10). It is clear that a person who has no means of survival will not be able to feed his family.

Someone who always demands money and material things from day one of the relationship

It is a danger sign if one party is always getting money and/or material things from the other party under one excuse or the other without ever reciprocating. If a person dumps all their financial obligations on you simply because you started a relationship there is a problem. The lady especially should not dump all her financial obligations – accommodation, clothing, hairdo, school fees, feeding, and siblings/parents – on the man.
