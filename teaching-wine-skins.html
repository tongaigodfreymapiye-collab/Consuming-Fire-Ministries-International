Old and New Wine Skins

By Apostle T. G. Mapiye

We are not partakers of the old wine of the dispensation of God’s wrath and condemnation but of the new wine of God’s pardon and grace. The justice between God and mankind was settled at the cross. This was symbolized by Jesus’ last supper with the disciples when he gave them bread to eat and wine to drink representing his body for the sacrifice for sins and his blood for redemption and forgiveness which was later fulfilled in his death, burial and resurrection. Thus, divine justice was met for divine grace to be poured over the whole world to all mankind.

“No one uses a new piece of cloth to patch old clothes. The patch would shrink and tear a bigger hole. No one pours new wine into old wineskins. The wine would swell and burst the old skins. Then the wine would be lost, and the skins would be ruined. New wine must be put into new wineskins. Both the skins and the wine will then be safe.” [Mat 9:16-17 CEV] Jesus spoke in a metaphor here to make his hearers understand a spiritual principle regarding to spiritual teachings. Jesus meant that you can not mix the Old Testament and the New Testament because they have different functions. His teaching was new and needed new mindsets, new hearts or new vessels that could contain it and keep it safe. Those who refused to change from their old mindsets to adapt to the new teaching Jesus brought failed to receive anything from Him.
