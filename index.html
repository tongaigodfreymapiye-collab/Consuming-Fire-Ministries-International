Who we are

A ministry born out of God's grace, love and power.

We exist to proclaim the Gospel, teach the Word faithfully.

The name Consuming Fire Ministries International (CFMI) comes from the following Bible passage:

“For our God is a consuming fire.”Hebrews 12:29

This church was founded by Apostle Tongai Godfrey Mapiye, together with Pastor Joyce Mapiye in October 2014. Apostle T. G. Mapiye was born again on 30 December 2002 following a personal encounter with the Lord Jesus Christ. After 12 years of preparation, Consuming Fire Ministries International (CFMI) was established as a Bible-based church committed to demonstrating the power of God in practice and leading people to salvation through the preaching and teaching of the Gospel of Jesus Christ. The church began in a 2 roomed cottage and has been impacting many lives and producing disciples for Jesus Christ to the glory of God with countless testimonies of healing, deliverance and transformed lives. We believe that we are simply continuing the same work left by the apostles in Bible days, expanding the Kingdom of God through the gospel of Jesus Christ.

The Church exists to communicate the whole will of God as recorded in the Bible to promote the personal and communal spiritual growth of Christians, to teach Christians to obey all that Christ commanded, and to train Christians for effective ministry and service to our Church, our community, our nation and globally (Ephesians 4:11-13; Matthew 18:20; 2 Timothy 2:2).

Mission statement

We endeavour to serve the purpose of God in our generation of reconciling the world to God through the preaching of the gospel of Jesus Christ and maturing Disciples of Christ through teaching and impartation of the Holy Spirit until the Lord returns.
