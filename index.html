Who we are

A ministry shaped by His presence.

We exist to proclaim the Gospel, teach the Word faithfully, nurture believers, and serve families and communities with compassion.

Our desire is to see people encounter Jesus Christ, grow in biblical understanding, and live out their faith through worship, prayer, family life, work, and service.
