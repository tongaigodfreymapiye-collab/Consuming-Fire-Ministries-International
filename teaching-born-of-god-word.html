We Are Born of God by the Word of God

By Apostle T. G. Mapiye

The Power of God’s Word in the New Birth

The Bible declares, “You have been born again, not of perishable seed, but of imperishable, through the living and enduring word of God.” (1 Peter 1:23 NIV) This Word is the very seed that plants new life deep within your spirit. It is not a lifeless word but a powerful force that initiates the miracle of spiritual rebirth.

The Living and Eternal Nature of God’s Word

God’s Word is described as living and active, sharper than any double-edged sword. It penetrates deep into your soul, discerning your thoughts and intentions, and is able to transform your heart and mind (Hebrews 4:12 NIV). It is not merely ancient text but the very breath of God that works powerfully in you every day.

Becoming a New Creation in Christ

When you experience this new birth, you become a completely new creation. The old self with its sinful ways passes away, and a fresh, holy life begins (2 Corinthians 5:17 NIV). This transformation is not just external but deep within, affecting your desires, your character, and your destiny.

The Role of Scripture in Spiritual Regeneration

To grow and mature in this new life, Scripture must be your daily nourishment. Reading, hearing, and meditating on God’s Word strengthens your faith and helps you walk in the fullness of your new identity in Christ. The Word is a constant source of spiritual regeneration, keeping you rooted and grounded as you continue your journey with God.
