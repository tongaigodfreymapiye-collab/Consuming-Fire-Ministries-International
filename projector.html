🎥 Microsoft Teams video conferencing

Start or join a Teams meeting, then show the meeting on the projector display. Teams blocks embedding its live meeting inside third-party pages, so the call opens in a separate Teams tab/window.

Project the call: Connect your projector as a second display (Windows: Win + P → Extend). Open Teams, join the meeting, and move the Teams window to the projector display; keep CFMI Projector Studio on your laptop. In Teams, use Share to present slides or content to meeting participants. For an in-room projector showing remote participants, put the Teams meeting window itself on the projector display.

Meeting links are saved in this browser only. A Teams account or guest access may be required. Camera and microphone permissions are managed by Teams and your browser.

Service order

    Preview / Live workflow: Select any library item to preview it here. Preview changes do not go live automatically. Click “Open live window” once, move it to your projector using Extended Display, then click “GO LIVE: send preview” only when ready. Prepare the next item in preview while the current live item remains on screen; click GO LIVE again to take the next item live.
    PowerPoint note: reliable live PowerPoint control requires Microsoft PowerPoint or a dedicated desktop integration. This browser-based starter supports local preview for images and common audio/video formats, PDF preview where the browser supports it, and service-order management. It does not claim to control the PowerPoint desktop application.
