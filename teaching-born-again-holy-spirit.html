We Are Born Again by the Power of the Holy Spirit

By Apostle T. G. Mapiye
“But when the kindness and the love of God our Savior toward man appeared, not by (our) works of righteousness which we have done, but according to His mercy He saved us, by the washing of regeneration (the new birth) and renewing by the Holy Spirit, whom He abundantly poured out on us through Jesus Christ our Savior.”

— Titus 3:4-6 (AMP)

This passage reveals that God’s salvation is not earned by our own righteous deeds or efforts but is a gracious gift born out of His mercy and love. The “washing of regeneration” refers to the spiritual cleansing and rebirth that the Holy Spirit brings about, making us new creations. This renewing work is poured out abundantly through Jesus Christ, who makes this transformation possible.

Jesus explained to Nicodemus, “That which is born of the flesh is flesh (that which is born physically is physical); and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit.”

— John 3:6 (AMP)

In other words, natural human birth produces a natural, physical life, but to enter the kingdom of God, one must be born spiritually by the Spirit of God. This spiritual birth is a divine work that changes the very nature of a person’s being.

Paul further explains, “But the person who is joined (united and linked closely) to the Lord is one spirit with Him.”

— 1 Corinthians 6:17 (AMP)

The new birth unites us to Christ in a mysterious but real way — our spirit becomes one with His Spirit, forming a deep, inseparable bond.

Moreover, the Apostle Paul assures us in Romans:

“But you are not in the flesh (controlled by the sinful nature), but in the Spirit (controlled by the Holy Spirit), if indeed the Spirit of God dwells in you. Now if anyone does not have the Spirit of Christ, he does not belong to Him… But if Christ (the Messiah) is in you, though your body is dead because of sin, the Spirit is life because of righteousness. And if the Spirit of Him who raised Jesus from the dead dwells in you, He who raised Christ Jesus from the dead will also give life to your mortal (physical) bodies through His Spirit who indwells you… For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are the sons of God.”

— Romans 8:9-11,14 (AMP)

This passage explains that being born again is not simply a one-time event but the beginning of a life led and empowered by the Spirit of God. The Spirit’s presence guarantees our identity as God’s children and promises resurrection life — not only spiritually but ultimately bodily as well. To be “led by the Spirit” means to live under His guidance, influence, and power as sons and daughters of God.

The new birth is the divine miracle by which God’s Spirit cleanses, renews, and joins us to Christ, making us His children, empowering us to live a transformed life full of hope and eternal promise.

The phrase “born again” literally means “born from above.” When Nicodemus came to Jesus by night, he expressed a genuine spiritual need—a deep need for a change of heart, a spiritual transformation. Jesus explained that this new birth is essential for entering the kingdom of God.

The new birth, or being born again, is a divine act of God whereby eternal life is imparted to the person who believes in Him. This spiritual rebirth results in becoming a new creation in Christ. The Scriptures affirm this truth:

  • “Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; the old has passed away, behold, the new has come.”

— 2 Corinthians 5:17 (AMP)

  • “He saved us, not by works of righteousness that we have done, but according to His mercy, through the washing of regeneration and renewing of the Holy Spirit.”

— Titus 3:5 (AMP)

  • “Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! According to His great mercy, He has caused us to be born again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead.”

— 1 Peter 1:3 (AMP)

  • “Everyone who practices righteousness is born of Him.”

— 1 John 2:29 (AMP)

  • “Whoever is born of God does not continue to sin.”

— 1 John 3:9 (AMP)

  • “Beloved, let us love one another, for love is from God; everyone who loves is born of God and knows God.”

— 1 John 4:7 (AMP)

  • “Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ is born of God.”

— 1 John 5:1 (AMP)

  • “We know that anyone born of God does not continue to sin.”

— 1 John 5:18 (AMP)

John 1:12-13 explains further that to be born again also means to become children of God through faith and trust in the name of Jesus Christ:

  • “But to all who did receive Him, who believed in His name, He gave the right to become children of God—who were born, not of blood nor of the will of the flesh nor of the will of man, but of God.”

— John 1:12-13 (AMP)
