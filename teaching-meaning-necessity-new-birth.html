The Meaning and Necessity of the New Birth

By Apostle T. G. Mapiye

Born Again Means Born From Above

“Born again” literally means “born from above,” a supernatural transformation by God (John 3:3-7). To be “born again” is to experience a divine transformation initiated by God Himself. The phrase actually means “born from above”—a supernatural work of the Holy Spirit that ushers one into the kingdom of God. Jesus said to Nicodemus,
“I assure you and most solemnly say to you, unless a person is born again [reborn from above—spiritually transformed, renewed, sanctified], he cannot [ever] see and experience the kingdom of God.”(John 3:3, AMP)

This new birth is not about religion or human effort; it is a heavenly encounter that radically changes the human heart and opens our eyes to the reality of God's kingdom.

Why We Need a New Birth: The Problem of Sin

Sin separates us from God and causes spiritual death. Only a new birth can restore the relationship (Romans 3:23, 6:23). The reason we must be born again lies in the universal condition of mankind—sin. Sin is not just wrongdoing; it is a spiritual disease that separates us from the life of God. Scripture reminds us,

“Since all have sinned and continually fall short of the glory of God,”

(Romans 3:23, AMP)

and again,

“For the wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God [that is, His remarkable, overwhelming gift of grace to believers] is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.”

(Romans 6:23, AMP)

No one can bridge the gap between themselves and God through good works or human morality. Only by being born of God can that separation be removed and reconciliation established.

The Spiritual Death and New Life in Christ

Without the new birth, we remain spiritually dead. Being born again brings new life and eternal security. Without the new birth, we are spiritually dead—cut off from the presence and power of God. But through Christ, a new life begins. When a person accepts Jesus and is born again, they receive His Spirit and become alive to God.

“Therefore if anyone is in Christ [that is, grafted in, joined to Him by faith in Him as Savior], he is a new creature [reborn and renewed by the Holy Spirit]; the old things [the previous moral and spiritual condition] have passed away. Behold, new things have come [because spiritual awakening brings a new life].”

(2 Corinthians 5:17, AMP)

This transformation is not symbolic—it is real, permanent, and empowered by the Spirit of God. It brings the assurance of eternal life and the indwelling presence of Christ.

Becoming Children of God Through Faith

Those who receive Jesus become God’s children and heirs of His promises (John 1:12, Romans 8:16-17). The new birth is not just an inward change; it also grants us a new identity. Through faith in Jesus Christ, we are not only cleansed—we are adopted. We become part of God's family and share in His inheritance.

“But to as many as did receive and welcome Him, He gave the right [the authority, the privilege] to become children of God, that is, to those who believe in (adhere to, trust in, and rely on) His name.”

(John 1:12, AMP)

And again,

“The Spirit Himself testifies and confirms together with our spirit [assuring us] that we [believers] are children of God. And if [we are His] children, [then we are His] heirs also: heirs of God and fellow heirs with Christ [sharing His spiritual blessing and inheritance].”

(Romans 8:16–17a, AMP)

This new position brings intimacy with the Father, access to His promises, and the guarantee of a glorious future.
