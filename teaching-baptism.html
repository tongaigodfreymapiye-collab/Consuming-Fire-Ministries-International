Baptismal Procedure

By Apostle T. G. Mapiye

BAPTISM BY IMMERSION

We believe that baptism is by immersion because of the following reasons:

  1. Baptism requires water. (Matthew 3:11)
  2. Baptism required plenty of water. (John 3:30)
  3. Baptism requires going down into the water. (Acts 8:30)
  4. Baptism requires coming up out of the water. (Matthew 3:16, Acts 8:39)

Water Baptism is an important spiritual landmark in every Christian’s life. It is one of the few ordinances that Christians are expected to perform. It is also important to have a good understanding of the biblical and spiritual significance of this event. Baptism is the immersion of a believer in water by a faithful local church in the name of Jesus. It is the outward expression of an inward change. Baptism signifies the essential facts of redemption, the death, burial, resurrection.

INSTRUCTIONS FOR DRESSING FOR BAPTISM

  1. Decent clothes must be worn (not transparent).
  2. Ladies having their menstrual periods should not come for baptism.
  3. Ladies are not to wear only underwear, underpants or underpants and bra when coming for baptism but must be fully dressed.
  4. No swimming costumes should be allowed.
  5. No bare-chested ladies should be allowed.
  6. All ladies must be fully covered.
  7. All ladies must wear bras under their blouses.
  8. Ladies and men must not change their clothes in the view of each other.
  9. Bring extra clothes to change after being baptized.

1. OPENING PRAYER

Begin with a prayer.

2. TEACH ABOUT THE REASONS FOR BAPTISM

We are gathered here today to baptize these believers according to the Word of God. The basis for baptism is:

  1. To obey the Word of God.
  2. It is one of the requirements after being Born Again. Acts 8:36–38.
  3. It is an outward symbol of what has happened to you spiritually. Colossians 2:12.
  4. To fulfill all righteousness. Matthew 3:15–16.

INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE BAPTIZER

  1. The baptizer should not be bare-chested.
  2. The baptizer must be fully and properly dressed.
  3. The baptizer must be a Holy Spirit-filled believer.

ASK THE BAPTISMAL CANDIDATE THE FOLLOWING QUESTIONS

  1. Are you Born Again?
  2. Have you received Christ as your personal Saviour?
  3. Do you believe that Jesus died for you on the cross and that He rose again?
  4. Do you believe that Jesus Christ will be coming again to take His church away?

If you get a negative answer to any of the following questions:

  1. Abort the Baptismal ceremony immediately.
  2. Lead the individual to accept Christ as his Saviour.
  3. You may continue to baptize the individual if he genuinely receives the Lord as Saviour.

INSTRUCTIONS ON IMMERSION

  1. Tell the candidate to hold nose and close eyes.
  2. Baptizer holds candidate with one hand behind candidate, and the other hand on the forehead.
  3. Baptizer fully immerses candidate in water and raises him up again.

IMMERSE THE BAPTISMAL CANDIDATE FULLY IN THE WATER

Ensure that he is fully submerged. (Ask him to hold his/her breath and his/her nose as he/she is immersed.)

AFTER THE CANDIDATE HAS CHANGED

Have a brief worship time. Thank the Lord.
