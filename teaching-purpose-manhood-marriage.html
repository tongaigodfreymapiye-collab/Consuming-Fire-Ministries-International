God’s Purpose for Manhood and Marriage

By Apostle T. G. Mapiye

“Do not seek a wife until you are first a man—stable, grounded, and submitted to God.” — Watchman Nee

In counselling many young men, I have come to realise that our generation is saturated with information but starving for wisdom. Marriage has become a mystery to many, but I believe God is raising a generation of men who will not only seek wives but also prepare themselves to become husbands after God’s own heart.

The first woman, Eve, was brought to Adam by God. Adam did not chase her or manipulate the situation. He was in the presence of God, working and walking in obedience. When the time was right, God brought his bride to him. God has not changed; He remains the same.

Some young men think they must first go to church and then get married in the church. They seem to forget that God did not create man and place him inside a church building. Marriage is not a reward for spiritual or religious activity; it is a platform for covenant responsibility. Marriage was never designed for reckless and irresponsible people.

Before asking, “Where is my bride?” you must ask, “Who am I as a man in God’s design?” Adam received his assignment before he received his bride. He was placed in a garden, called to work, and commanded to obey. Only after that did God say, “It is not good that the man should be alone” (Genesis 2:18).

“So the Lord God took the man [He had made] and settled him in the Garden of Eden to cultivate and keep it” (Genesis 2:15, AMP).

God made man and gave him work before He gave him a wife. That is the divine order: purpose before partnership, assignment before affection. Any man who avoids work but seeks a woman is already out of divine alignment.

Before Adam could have sexual relations with Eve and start a family, God gave him a divine assignment. In other words, every young man must understand why God created him, discover his assignment, and learn to be fruitful in that assignment before thinking about starting a family.

A man’s sexual needs are not the first thing God addresses as he grows towards maturity. A man must first discover his assignment and become established in it. This is an important foundation for his manhood.

God’s purpose for marriage was never to complete a man’s brokenness but to complement his wholeness. You were not created to chase women; you were created to pursue God. Marriage is not for boys; it is for men who have encountered God, accepted responsibility, and understood the meaning of covenant.

Salvation: The Foundation of Purpose

Before a man can walk in his divine assignment, he must be spiritually reborn. Salvation is the foundation of purpose. Until a young man gives his life to Christ, he will struggle to understand the reason for his existence.

Just as Adam was created by God and placed in Eden, a place where he experienced God’s presence, every young man must return to God through Jesus Christ. Only through salvation can a man discover his true identity, assignment, and authority. A man without Christ may find a wife, but he will struggle to lead her spiritually.

Salvation aligns a man with God’s original purpose and empowers him to walk in holiness, responsibility, and fruitfulness. Marriage is not a substitute for salvation, and sex is not a cure for emptiness. A man who is saved is strengthened by God. He can become a safe place for his bride, loving and leading her with godly character.

A Call to Young Men

Young man, are you busy working the ground God has given you? Are you cultivating your gifts, growing in wisdom, and remaining in His presence?

Many young men live disorderly lives because they have not embraced discipline, responsibility, and godly direction.

Too many men want women to submit to them when they have not yet submitted themselves to God. You cannot properly care for someone else’s heart while you are still broken within. You cannot effectively lead a family if you do not know where God is taking you.

Before you seek a wife, seek God. Before you desire to lead a family, learn to submit to God’s leadership. Before you ask someone to share your life, take responsibility for the life God has entrusted to you.

A Prayer for Young Men

Dear Lord,

Before I find my wife, help me to find You. Make me a man of vision, purity, wisdom, and strength. Teach me to walk in obedience, fulfil my divine assignment, and accept responsibility. Prepare my heart and character so that, when I find my bride, I will be ready to love her as Christ loves the Church.

In Jesus’ name, Amen.
